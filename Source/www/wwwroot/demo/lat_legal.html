Latin Legal Analysis Features

This module uses a specialized grammar for Roman-law construction types and classical legal Latin:

Analysis of ablative absolute constructions, gerundives, and supine phrases common in legal Latin.
Detection of subjects and objects in subjunctive clauses of obligation and stipulation.
Mapping of legal entity types: personae (parties), obligationes (obligations), actiones (legal actions), res (subject matter).
Cross-referencing Roman jurisprudential concepts: contractus, stipulatio, legatum, tutela.
