Paste your text

How it works

The tool uses syntactic analysis to extract the narrative skeleton from your text:

Characters — subjects identified by the parser (who performs actions)
Actions — predicates and main verbs (what happens)
Targets — direct objects (who/what receives the action)
Settings — adverbial phrases (where/when it happens)

Export the result as TXT and use it as a game scenario skeleton for writing quests, dialogues, and cutscenes.
